REQUIRED TO MONITOR YOUR HEALTH

Your health profile

Keel needs these details to keep exercise safe and to adapt intensity in real time. Your data is uploaded only to your private Keel database and is never shared with other apps.
1 Full name & date of birth
2 Biological sex
3 Height & weight
4 Resting & max heart rate
5 Mobility / body profile
6 Emergency contact
7 Consent to sensor & location access

PERMISSIONS
Sensor & location access
Keel only reads the sensors you approve. You can revoke access any time in Menu → App permissions.
REQUIRED DEVICES
Health & Fitness data from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) is uploaded to your private Keel database only. It is never shared with other apps or third parties.
INSTALL KEEL
Add to Home Screen
Install KEEL to get instant access, offline support, and a full-screen experience — just like a native app. After installing, KEEL will ask for location access to enable the live map.

About KEEL
WHAT IS KEEL
An adaptive training companion that keeps exercise safe and steady. Sessions are matched to your readiness, movement profile, and daily energy — so you train with intention, not guesswork.
